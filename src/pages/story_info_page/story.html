Omar Marmoush, Randal Kolo Muani, Lloyd Kelly: Eight stars set to make their UEFA Champions League debuts

The January transfer window was a busy one for some of Europe's biggest clubs, including Manchester City and Juventus, who are both in action in this week's UEFA Champions League play-offs.

Several new signings will likely feature for both clubs, while Paris Saint-Germain and Celtic also made notable additions as they look to reach the Champions League last 16.

The UEFA Champions League returns this week as the new-look format enters the knockout stage, beginning with the play-offs.
